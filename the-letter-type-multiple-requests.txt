[Biến thể cho phù hợp – Multiple requests]

Sample 1: You have seen an advertisement (quảng cáo) for an international internship program where participants can learn about culture, marketing, and other professional skills. Write an email to apply for this internship, explaining why you are interested, what skills you have that make you a good candidate, and asking any relevant questions.
___
Dear Sir,
How are you? How is your work? I hope you are well. I am so happy to write to you. Today, I am writing this email to apply for this internship.
Firstly, I have seen an advertisement for an international internship program. I think it is very useful and meaningful because I can learn about culture, marketing, and other professional skills.
Secondly, I really like it because it helps me widen knowledge and learn many useful things such as experience, new information, new skill, and so on. It can help me make much money, find a good job, and have a high salary in the feature.
Finally, I want ask you about some other benefits of this program such as the contact, relationships, activities.
